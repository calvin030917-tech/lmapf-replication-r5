# Benchmark, analytical comparators and style

- Moving AI MAPF benchmarks, https://www.movingai.com/benchmarks/mapf.html. Downloaded warehouse variants 1 and 2 from the public index at https://www.movingai.com/benchmarks/mapf/index.html. Original archives: https://www.movingai.com/benchmarks/mapf/warehouse-10-20-10-2-1.map.zip and https://www.movingai.com/benchmarks/mapf/warehouse-10-20-10-2-2.map.zip. The bytes of the extracted map files are unchanged. The SHA-256 pins are in the [R5 protocol](../r5/protocol_r5.json).
- Zheng et al., learning-guided priority analysis, [Sec. 6 in the author HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23838v1), official publication https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.1.20611. Sections 6.1 and 6.2 used for the description of same-state order samples, planned movements and strategy change. Our fixed plan-observation proxies are explicitly specified.
- Phillips and Likhachev, [SIPP](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~maxim/files/sipp_icra11.pdf), especially Sections IV–V. Used for interval-state terminology. The independently implemented rolling application in this archive is defined in its own code.
- Li et al., [RHCR](https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/17344/17344-13-20838-1-2-20210518.pdf), Section 4 and local deadlock/progress discussion. Asano et al., [NCF2](https://ifmas.csc.liv.ac.uk/Proceedings/aamas2023/pdfs/p887.pdf), Figures and pp. 888–891. Morag et al., [PCS/PC-MAPF](https://harabor.net/data/papers/mzkcfhs-socs24-ppwg.pdf), pp. 82–84, 88–89.
