# R4 validation protocol

The specification below was written before running or inspecting the R4 holdout data. The coordinator, safety checks, observation horizon H=32, capped times and three event definitions are those of R3. The promotion moves the focal robot to the front for a single cycle. Other ranks, issued tasks, seed, budget and tie breaking are fixed. The two branches then resume congestion ordering. These are scenario-level simulation checks of a diagnostic, with no target event rate and no power claim. Each event is retained even when its count is zero.

## Matched opposition groups on the initial layouts

Use original single and bypass layouts with 24 robots and local congestion ordering, at B=120 and B=336, using source runs with seeds 4101–4116. The candidate ticks are 48, 64, 80, 96, 112, 128, 144 and 160. At each tick, choose the active cross-strip robot of largest relative age, tied by ID, among robots near the strip (x=4–12) that have not reached the destination bank, were issued the goal at least two steps earlier and did not reduce static remaining distance on the preceding action. The same rule applies regardless of nearby traffic direction. Discard a tick if no such robot exists or if that focal robot is already first. Mark whether a cross-strip robot bound for the opposing bank is also near the strip, has not reached its destination bank and is within Manhattan distance four. Record every retained tick. Report counts and event rates overall, by layout and budget, and by baseline rank 2–4, 5–8, 9+. The two groups are observational. Bootstrap paired-by-scenario strata (seeds are shared across the two budgets for a map). Report a raw event-3 rate difference and a sensitivity contrast standardized to strata with both groups, weighted by total observed ticks in those strata. Sparse groups are reported with their denominators. The population observed is eligible scheduled ticks, not all cycles or all traffic.

## Independent layouts and goals under two seed constructions

Holdout layouts are 21×13 synthetic grids. Three aisles cross the middle strip at rows 2, 6 and 10 in Ladder. Offset uses components with entrances on different rows joined by short vertical corridors. Shelves, strip x=9–11, connectivity and depots are defined in validation.py. Use 24 robots, both B=120 and B=336, seeds 5101–5112, matched over both seed constructors and budgets on each layout. Simulate 320 steps. Each robot draws its target from the relevant bank using pre-generated sequences independent of the policy. Cross-bank probability alternates between 0.65 and 0.35 in four-job blocks, starting with 0.65, with a different PRNG key from R3. Every 16 steps from 64 through 288 choose an eligible focal using the same nonprogressing cross-strip rule, the strip approaches extended to x=6–14. Log every scheduled eligible tick and opposition label. The source policy is local congestion in both constructions.

The recursive constructor is the R3 seed. The serial constructor builds eight reservation-safe actions for each robot in fixed age order within the window. At each layer reserve all current cells as waits. Each robot releases its own wait and picks the smallest (static distance, cell ID) among legal actions. Only agents processed earlier can have freed their cells. Exclude reverse edge use, and keep waiting available. This algorithm has no recursive reassignment and yields different provisional paths. The weighted A* replacement rule, budget charging and goal-hold semantics remain common. Report each map × budget × constructor, event counts, capped effect, other-robot outcomes and source replays. Eligible ticks are allowed to differ between constructors. For an additional controlled comparison, at each selected tick from a recursive source run apply both constructors to that identical state in both branches, then use the original source constructor after two steps. The outcome estimand is agreement of each diagnostic event across two seed constructors on the same snapshot, and rates may differ.
