# R5 validation design

The settings in protocol_r5.json and this description were recorded before any outcomes on seeds 6101–6116 and 7101–7116 were read. There is no model fitting. The validation question is what an observer can identify after a one-robot move-to-front when given a plan versus an executed replay and local feasibility controls. Outcome counts, unfavorable cases, and sparse cells are all reported.

Two complete Moving AI warehouse files are used unmodified. Only demand is localized. Choose the two dense aisle rows nearest each side of the center latitude displaced by nine rows, and central columns within 11 of the midpoint. Draw starts without replacement, then assign individual coupled goal sequences (80% opposite pool, 20% same pool, no identical consecutive goal). Robot ID and phase key the RNG. The same map/fleet/scenario carries the same demand across caps and policies. Four-neighbor actions, one robot per cell, no opposing edge in one timestep. At a goal arrival, issue the next task immediately and finish executing the already committed two-step prefix.

The new planner is a separately implemented rolling prioritized safe-interval planner. It uses a rotating-tie dependency seed, sparse reservations, earliest arrivals in a cell's collision-free intervals, and coverage of the full window. It does not import any R4 planning code. A shared per-round cap debits SIPP interval-state expansions. Record seed move tests, SIPP expansion counts, edge-time tests and reservation writes separately. Time the full cycle up to legal commit, including priority computation and safety validation, in serial independent workload measurements.

Use local-density ordering at source. At predetermined ticks, choose the highest relative-age nonlead robot with no reduction in last-step remaining distance, a current remaining distance of at most 24, and an active task at least two steps old. Rank and candidate lookahead are computed before the resource label. A resource match requires both the same undirected edge on two deterministic reservation-free six-step routes, opposite traversal directions, and approach indices differing by at most two. Gate contention is an intent marker on an actual common edge, regardless of alternative routes. After selection, sample up to two ticks per resource label per run by seeded uniform sampling without replacement. The target population is these selected snapshots. Cluster by the independent seed within map/fleet, retaining both budgets; sparse strata are described per cell. The pooled descriptive difference gets a cluster interval only if each label occurs in at least 12 independent scenario keys. The contrast is observational.

Plan-only comparison follows the matched-state order and planned-direction view of Zheng et al. §6. Source order, focal-first order and six random permutations are generated in the same state with the same planner and cap. Both measured branches contribute planned next headings, direction relative to static goal distance, and improvement in two-step remaining distance. A directional proxy predicts earlier focal completion when the first planned action improves from retreat to wait or goalward movement, or from wait to goalward movement. A second proxy predicts improvement when the focal two-step endpoint has smaller remaining distance after promotion. These are specified diagnostic comparators, not a rerun of the learned RL policy or a claim that the source paper predicts service. Rank/direction summaries also group alternative mechanism flags without requiring the comparator to label causes. The registered outcomes are earlier focal capped completion and earlier focal capped completion with at least one other robot delayed. Score them independently from full executed cell paths and issued goals. The original-order replay must reproduce each source action. Keep true finishes exactly at the cap separate from unfinished tasks.

For every evaluated focal query in both arms, probe four local mechanisms and check the result independently by explicit time-layer enumeration: (B) the actual goal query was cut or skipped, returned no goal and an uncapped hold-goal witness exists in the same reservation and horizon; (R) the goal cannot be touched with a legal continuation by the horizon with reservations, but can with no reservations; (G) releasing the goal yields earlier feasible first arrival than holding, including infeasible holding; (W) no legal release arrival in the original window, and one appears under an eight-tick extension whose supplied reservation paths wait at their original endpoints. Multiple flags can coexist. They are local counterfactual properties and do not apportion total closed-loop effects. An all-zero flag vector is permitted. Synthetic fixtures target individual flags and are verified with the time-layer reference. Candidate returned, candidate equals seed and actually executed prefix equals seed are counted separately.

A second set of new paired seeds compares the historical two-stage and longest remaining-distance rules under the same new planner. It is supplementary. Report paired scenario rate/exposure contrasts and their intervals in the eight layout/fleet/cap settings, with no operational noninferiority claim. The prespecified aligned service cohort is task index 0 (released at tick 0 in both arms), followed for 64 steps; pair at the scene level. Length strata from continuously replenished workloads describe each policy's realized task composition. Serial timing uses the first diagnostic seed in each setting. R4 historical records and seeds remain archived separately.
